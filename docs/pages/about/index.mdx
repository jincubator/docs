# John Whitton and Jincubator

Jincubator is the editorial and engineering identity for selected independent
systems work led by John Whitton. It is not presented as a staffed agency,
fund, customer portfolio, or partnership network.

For professional history, résumé, and availability, visit
[johnwhitton.com](https://johnwhitton.com/). Historical Jincubator material is
kept separately in the [Archive](/archive).
